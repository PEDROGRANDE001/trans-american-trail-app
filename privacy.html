Trans-American Trail: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Trans-American Trail ("the app") is published by Higney International, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and almost everything it knows about you stays on your phone.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We do not receive it.

  • Recorded tracks and trips. When you tap Record Track, or start a trip with Start my TAT, the app records your position while you ride, including with the screen off, so it can draw the route you took and count your trip miles. Tracks and trip stats are saved on your device.
  • Your trip records. Daily log entries, kit and gear lists, items marked as purchased, rider reports and the photo you attach to a report, saved tracks, and your home screen layout.
  • Settings. Your display preferences and the fact that you accepted the Terms of Service.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We cannot recover it for you.

2. Location

The app asks for two kinds of location permission:

  • While using the app: to show your position on the route map and to place a hazard report where you are standing.
  • Always (background): only while you are recording a track with Record Track or have started a trip with Start my TAT, so the app keeps recording your ride and counting your trip miles with the screen off. It stops when you stop the track, or pause or finish the trip. Background location is not used for anything else and is not sent to us.

You can refuse either permission or change it at any time in your phone's Settings. The app still works without location; the map simply cannot show where you are, and tracks and trips cannot be recorded.

3. Information you choose to share with other riders

Hazard reports. If you report a hazard (for example a washout, closed gate or high water), the app sends the hazard type, its status, your optional note, the map coordinates of the hazard, the US state and the time to our server, and other riders using the app can see it. Hazard reports do not include your name, an account, a device identifier or your recorded track. Please do not put personal information in the note.

Sharing a track. If you use Share on a saved track, your phone's share sheet sends your current position and that route to the person or app you choose. That goes directly from your phone; we do not receive a copy.

Suggestions. The Make a Suggestion screen opens your own mail app with your message filled in. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to read and answer your suggestion.

4. Information the app downloads

The app downloads trail information (gear and bike upgrade catalogs, destinations and hazard reports) from our database, which is hosted by Supabase. It downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap. It checks for app updates through Expo. When your phone connects to any of these services, the service receives technical information that every internet connection carries, such as your IP address and the app version, and may keep it in its server logs for a short time. We do not use this information to identify you.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts or logins.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, or share it with anyone for marketing.

6. Service providers

We use the following providers to run the app. Each processes data only to provide its service:

  • Supabase (database hosting for hazard reports and trail information)
  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles)
  • Expo (delivery of app updates)
  • Apple (app distribution through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy)

7. Keeping and deleting data

Information on your device stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Hazard reports stay visible to riders while they are useful and may be removed when they are out of date. To ask us to remove a hazard report you submitted, write to us at the address below and tell us its location and date.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Safety information

The app is a planning and navigation aid and not an emergency service. Features that open your phone dialer or maps app hand you over to those apps; we do not receive what you do there.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date above. A change that affects information already collected will be explained in the app before it applies.
